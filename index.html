Research

01
NYU WirelessSpring 2026

Discrete-time modelling of N-path receivers with interference

An end-to-end discrete-time simulator of an N-path receiver, including amplifier saturation and quantization. Derived closed-form input/output responses for signal and interference and benchmarked them against a conventional receiver.

  • N-path
  • Mixer-first Rx
  • Interference
02
NYU WirelessSummer 2025

Learning-based signal recovery in nonlinear RF systems

A learned MLVAMP algorithm with neural denoisers for recovering signals through nonlinear receivers under interference. Modelled the receiver impairments and built a hybrid inference framework around them. About 20 dB better than linear methods.

  • Bayesian inference
  • Deep unfolding
  • Message passing
03
NYU WirelessSummer 2024

Interpolation for fast channel estimation in ray tracing

Interpolation-based ray tracing to speed up channel modelling in Sionna, plus a reflection model for more accurate line-of-sight MIMO simulation and spherical-wavefront modelling.

  • mmWave
  • LOS MIMO
  • Sionna
04
IIT MadrasFall 2022

Sparse signal recovery for massive random access

Improved the sparse recovery stage of FASURA, a coding scheme for massive random access. Implemented SBL, MSBL, AMP-MSBL and GGAMP-MSBL from scratch.

  • Sparse recovery
  • SBL
  • AMP
